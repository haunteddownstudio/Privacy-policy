# Privacy Policy for Stranded at Night

**Effective Date:** October 2026

Stranded at Night is developed by **Haunted Down Studio**.

## Information Collection

Stranded at Night does not require players to create an account or provide personal information to play.

The game works fully offline. It does not use third-party services, analytics, advertising networks, or in-app purchases. No personal data is collected, transmitted, or shared.

Game progress (such as discovered endings) is stored only on your own device and never leaves it.

## Advertising

Stranded at Night contains no advertisements.

## In-App Purchases

Stranded at Night contains no in-app purchases.

## Children's Privacy

Stranded at Night is not directed toward children under 13.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
