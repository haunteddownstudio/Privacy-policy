# Privacy Policy for Critical Grave

**Effective Date:** August 2026

Critical Grave is developed by **Haunted Down Studio**.

## Information Collection

Critical Grave does not require players to create an account or provide personal information to play.

The game may use third-party services provided by Google, including:

- Google Play Services
- Google AdMob
- Google Play Billing (for optional in-app purchases)

These services may collect information such as device identifiers, diagnostics, and advertising information in accordance with Google's own privacy policies.

## Advertising

Critical Grave displays advertisements through Google AdMob.

## In-App Purchases

Critical Grave offers optional in-app purchases, including the Founder's Die. Purchases are processed through Google Play.

## Children's Privacy

Critical Grave is not directed specifically toward children under 13.

## Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.
